I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Division of Communication Systems, Department of Electrical Engineering (ISY), Linköping University, Sweden, working with Prof. Erik G. Larsson and Prof. Nicolò Michelusi.

My research lies at the intersection of optimization, distributed algorithms, and wireless communications. I am particularly interested in the design and theoretical analysis of communication-efficient algorithms for networked systems, with an emphasis on resource constraints, convergence guarantees, and practical applications.

My current research focuses on decentralized optimization, distributed consensus, and decentralized machine learning. Beyond theoretical algorithm development, I have experience in wireless communication systems, particularly intelligent reflecting surface (IRS)-assisted communications, including analytical research and laboratory and field experiments. These experiences motivate my interest in bridging mathematical optimization with practical wireless communication systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer and Information Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen) in November 2024, under the supervision of Prof. Zhi-Quan (Tom) Luo. Prior to that, I obtained my B.Sc. in Mathematical Sciences from Tongji University in 2018.
