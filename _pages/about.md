## About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Division of Communication Systems, Department of Electrical Engineering (ISY), Linköping University, Sweden, working with Prof. Erik G. Larsson and Prof. Nicolò Michelusi.

My research lies at the intersection of **optimization, distributed algorithms, and wireless communications**. I am particularly interested in the design and theoretical analysis of communication-efficient algorithms for networked systems, with an emphasis on resource constraints, convergence guarantees, and practical applications.

My current research focuses on decentralized optimization, distributed consensus, and decentralized machine learning. Beyond theoretical algorithm development, I have experience in wireless communication systems, particularly intelligent reflecting surface (IRS)-assisted communications, including analytical research and laboratory and field experiments. These experiences motivate my interest in bridging mathematical optimization with practical wireless communication systems.

I received my **Ph.D. in Computer and Information Engineering** from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen) in November 2024, under the supervision of Prof. Zhi-Quan (Tom) Luo. Prior to that, I obtained my **B.Sc. in Mathematical Sciences** from Tongji University in 2018.

---

## Research Interests

My research interests span four closely related directions:

- **Decentralized Optimization:** Developing and analyzing distributed optimization algorithms under communication and computational constraints, with particular interests in resource allocation, convergence analysis, and efficient communication and sampling strategies.

- **Wireless Communications:** Exploring optimization and algorithm design for wireless communication systems, building on my previous research in IRS-assisted communications and experience in experimental evaluation of wireless prototypes.

- **Distributed Consensus and Network Design:** Investigating consensus algorithms and communication topology design, including relay-assisted networks and efficient information exchange in distributed systems.

- **Decentralized Machine Learning:** Studying scalable decentralized optimization methods for machine learning and matrix optimization problems, combining theoretical convergence analysis with numerical evaluation.

## Teaching

I have teaching assistant experience in mathematics and statistics, including calculus, linear algebra, time series, and multivariate statistical methods.

I am also interested in developing graduate-level courses connecting theoretical foundations with modern applications in statistics and machine learning.
