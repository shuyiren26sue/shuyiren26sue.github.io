## Teaching Approach

My teaching interests lie in mathematics, statistics, optimization, and their applications.

I aim to connect mathematical foundations with practical problems, emphasizing conceptual understanding, the intuition behind theoretical results, and the assumptions under which these results hold.

For mathematically intensive subjects, I believe it is important to balance theoretical rigor with accessible explanations and examples that help students understand why the concepts matter.

## Course Development

**Non-asymptotic Bounds in High-Dimensional Statistics**  
Doctoral-level course under development

I am interested in developing a doctoral-level course introducing fundamental tools and results in modern high-dimensional statistics.

The planned course emphasizes concentration inequalities, statistical estimation, and applications to high-dimensional regression, principal component analysis, and matrix estimation.

The teaching approach combines theoretical foundations, proof intuition, and examples drawn from contemporary research.
